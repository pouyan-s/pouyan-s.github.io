		  <h4 style="color:#8ED530">Overview</h4>
		  <p>Sharing maps on proprietary platforms can be costly and often comes with storage limitations. To ensure open and sustainable access, I used the QGIS2Web plugin to export maps as Leaflet-based interactive web maps, then customized them with JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.</p>
      <!-- Content -->
      <div class="row">
		<!-- Left column -->
        <div class="6u 12u$(small)">
          <!--Rest of the overview-->
		  <p>This example from my "GIS for Community Archival Access" project showcases a georeferenced 1910 map of the Rosemount area (Weston, York, Ontario) for the Weston Historical Society. I implemented a click-based transparency toggle that allows community members to compare the historical map with the present landscape. In addition, I added a dismissible info panel and customized the default layer panel to improve usability. The final map was hosted independently on GitHub Pages and shared with community members, demonstrating both technical adaptability and commitment to accessible public history.</p>
